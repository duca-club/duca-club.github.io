## Event Details

In celebration of International Women in Cyber Day, DUCA is proud to partner with WiCyS (Women in Cybersecurity) to present a special digital forensics workshop!

**The Scenario:**
A student has gone missing. All we have is their locked laptop. You have 30 minutes to extract the data, piece together the clues, and figure out what happened.

What you'll learn:
- Fundamentals of digital forensics and incident response (DFIR)
- How to perform disk and memory image analysis
- Locating hidden clues, logs, and artifacts in a file system
- Collaborative problem solving in high-pressure scenarios

Bring your laptop and join us for this exciting challenge!
