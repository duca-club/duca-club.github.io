## Event Details

Looking to boost your resume and pass your first entry-level cybersecurity certification? Join us for an intensive study and exam prep session where we'll help you go from zero to ready.

What we'll do:
- Overview of industry certifications (CompTIA Security+, CCNA, ISC2 CC, etc.)
- Group study and breakdown of key exam concepts
- Guided practice tests with real-time explanations
- Tips and tricks on how to study effectively and pass on your first try

Boost your credentials and walk away with a clear roadmap to your next certification!
