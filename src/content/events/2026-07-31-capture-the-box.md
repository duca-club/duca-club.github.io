## Event Details

Get ready for an exciting Capture The Flag (CTF) competition! DUCA has partnered with Hack The Box (HTB) to deliver a custom CTF night.

What to expect:
- Jeopardy-style CTF challenges spanning Web, Pwn, Crypto, Forensics, and Reverse Engineering
- Team up with other members or compete solo
- Live scoreboard and hints to help beginners get started
- Awesome HTB swag and prizes for top-ranking teams

Food and drinks will be provided to keep you fueled. Bring your laptops!
