## Event Details

Are you wondering how to navigate the cybersecurity degree and break into the industry before graduation? Join two of your fellow senior students who have done exactly that!

In this workshop, we'll cover:
- What subjects to focus on and what topics you can speedrun
- Useful certifications vs. wastes of time
- Building a portfolio that employers actually care about
- Landing internships and entry-level positions

Bring your questions and get ready for some real advice.
