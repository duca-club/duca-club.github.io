## Event Details

Physical security is just as important as digital security! We've teamed up with the RMIT Cyber Security Club to bring you a hands-on lockpicking and physical security workshop.

What we'll cover:
- How locks work under the hood
- Introduction to lockpicking tools and techniques
- Exploring physical security bypasses and vulnerabilities
- Hands-on practice with standard padlock training kits

All tools and practice locks will be provided. No experience required!
