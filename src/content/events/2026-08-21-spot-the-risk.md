## Event Details

Cybersecurity isn't just about technical exploits; it's also about managing risk. We've partnered with ISACA Melbourne to host a hands-on risk assessment workshop!

What we'll do:
- Learn the basics of risk identification, analysis, and evaluation
- Walk through real-world threat modeling scenarios
- Work in teams to identify and rate risks in simulated infrastructure
- Learn how to present security risks to business stakeholders

Gain valuable governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) skills that are highly sought after by employers!
